# Auraliscope 2.0 RC 2 (build 118) — what's new since 2.0 (build 91)

App 2.0 rc 2 / build 118; Bridge VST3 and AU 2.0.118. A local release candidate: the bundles are ad hoc signed and the
installer is unsigned and not notarized.

## New in build 118
- **Multi-FFT Spectrum looks like the other panels.** The plot sits in the same inset well as the Spectrum card, with
  the same axis type, grid weights and label positions. Left/Right (and M/S) use the Waveform's channel colours, and
  the key moved into the title row like the Waveform's. The footer readout (update rate, window delay and a running
  "t" clock) is gone: the rate and delay are in the panel's settings, and the clock didn't match the DAW or file time.
- **Pre-release updates (opt-in).** Settings ▸ General ▸ Updates ▸ Include pre-releases also offers beta and
  release-candidate builds from GitHub. Off by default, so the stable channel is unchanged.

## Since build 91

### Analysis
- **Analyze Audio File… (⌘O)** measures a whole file (WAV, AIFF, CAF, MP3, AAC, ALAC, FLAC and more), mono to 9.1.6,
  against any delivery standard. It runs in the main window, and a five-minute file takes about two seconds.
- **Four-page loudness & master report** for live sessions (⌘E) and files, as a PDF or CSV.
- **Multi-FFT Spectrum (Beta)**, an optional Stereo panel with three FFT sizes fused into one calibrated curve. It has
  presets, Precision/Balanced/Responsive priorities, Tone or PSD scale and optional M/S. It can replace the Dock's
  spectrum, and its engine stops when the panel closes. Build 117 made the engine about 7× cheaper.
- The Spectrogram and Spectrum (local, Classic) can share one 2048/4096/8192 STFT. The original analysis stays
  available for A/B.

### Immersive
- **Your own speaker layouts:** place each speaker by azimuth, elevation and distance. Export or import them as JSON.
- **Routing follows Audio MIDI Setup**, plus a **Channel Order** menu (SMPTE/ITU, Film/Pro Tools, VST3/Nuendo/Cubase).
  This fixes surrounds that appeared at the front and 9.1.6 mixes that put channels in the wrong places.
- **Radiant body** is the default Soundstage style: each band glows where its speakers place it, with point lights.
  A **listener's head** shows which way is front. Immersive defaults to 9.1.6 until you choose a format.

### Layout and windows
- **Dock mode** (⌥⌘S) replaces the strip. Resize the gaps between its panels, minimize it, and split
  Spectrogram | Spectrum side by side.
- Show or hide panels from a toolbar list. Panels enlarge, detach and return more smoothly.
- **Settings presets** save as files in Application Support and can be duplicated, shown in Finder or moved to the Trash.

### Look and feel
- New design: frosted glass panels, round buttons, gentle motion (Reduce Motion turns it into fades) and a new app icon.
- **Themes:** Pearl (default), Sakura Pink, Milk Frog, Mist Lilac, Slate Blue, Cobalt, Glacier, Lagoon, Mint,
  Graphite and others, plus the hardware-style **Studio Console**.
- Glowing stereo Dots. The Loudness card no longer has an inner plate. Always on top starts off.
- **Six languages:** English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Korean, Japanese and Spanish.
- A three-page **welcome tutorial**.

### Everyday use
- Type the loudness target and true-peak ceiling directly (for example "−14.5 LUFS").
- Loudness presets use neutral names everywhere. The technical standards keep their names.
- **Check for Updates** from the app menu, About or Settings.
- **Feedback** links in Settings. The status bar shows the live display frame rate.
- **Smoother and lighter:** ProMotion 120 fps, even scrolling with large DAW buffers, no drawing for hidden panels, and
  an app about 44 % smaller.
- GPLv3 licensing, with third-party notices in the app, plugins, installer and source archive.

## Still not included
- Dialogue-gated loudness (the meter reads the full programme).
- Multi-FFT from the Bridge plugin (it needs local audio in this Beta).
- Restoring detached windows and Dock mode at launch; offline renders, Ambisonics, Auro and 22.2.

## Installing
Use `Auraliscope 2.0 rc 2 build 118.pkg` and choose the app, VST3 and/or Audio Unit. It replaces any 2.0 build in place
and keeps your settings. The app needs an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later; the plugins are universal.
