# Auraliscope 2.0 RC 3 (build 120) — what's new since 2.0 RC 2 (build 118)

App 2.0 rc 3 / build 120; Bridge VST3 and AU 2.0.120. **An internal QA build for private testing, not a release:** the
bundles are ad hoc signed and the installer is unsigned and not notarized. Build 119 was never packaged on its own; its
changes are part of build 120.

## New in build 120

### Capture
- **A capture that keeps failing restarts itself.** This covers a device that refuses every audio cycle (its sample
  rate changed and no notice came, for one) or delivers more audio per cycle than the capture holds.
  - **When:** after about a second of lost audio, Auraliscope restarts the capture at once, then 2 s and 8 s later if
    needed. The status line says "capture keeps failing · restarting it (attempt n of 3)".
  - **What counts as working:** a restart counts only when audio arrives again (silence counts).
  - **Your measurement:** at the same sample rate, integrated loudness, peaks and histories carry on across one
    marked break. At a new rate the measurement starts afresh, as a rate change always has.
  - **Giving up:** after three restarts that don't help, capture stops. The status names the reason and asks you to
    choose Start Capture; nothing restarts it by itself until you do, or choose another source.
  - **Cancelled by:** pause, a source change, unplugging or a device change.
  - **Unchanged:** normal rate changes, unplugging and pause. DAW plugin sources are not affected.
- **Lost audio is counted.** Audio a device refused, audio too long for the capture, and the cut-off end of an
  over-long output capture used to vanish silently. It is now counted as dropped and marked as one break where audio
  resumes, so the status bar's "Interrupted … s missing" shows it and loudness never joins audio across the gap.

### DAW plugin sources
- **Choosing between copies.** When Auraliscope finds your saved insert again by itself and a copy of it then appears
  (a duplicated track, the project open twice), it waits for you to choose, as the status says. It no longer picks one
  by itself a moment later.
- **Paused plugins resume when you leave them.** A plugin whose measurement you paused in Auraliscope resumes when you
  switch to another insert or a device, or stop. Before, it stayed paused in the plugin.

## New in build 119 (part of 120)

### Spectrum and spectrogram
- **Tilt is a slider:** 0 to 6 dB per octave in half-dB steps (it was three fixed choices), for the Spectrum and the
  spectrogram. Your previous choice carries over. Readings stay true.
- **Frequency scale:** Log (from 20 Hz, every octave the same room) or Linear (from 0 Hz, every hertz the same room).
  The analysis is the same.
- **Shared STFT:** Legacy (build 113's multi-resolution analysis) or a shared Hann STFT at a chosen size. Enhanced,
  Immersive and plugin sources keep their own analysis.

### Multi-FFT Spectrum (Beta)
- **Hover readout:** a frequency's pitch and each curve's level under the pointer.
- **Its own tilt slider and frequency scale.**
- **Reorganised settings:** preset and priority; display (scale, tilt, Mid and Side, smoothing); fusion; and each band
  with its FFT size, overlap, update ceiling and window. Every setting is explained.
- The Multi-FFT switch left the Spectrum's settings: show or hide the panel like any other.

### App
- **Welcome sheet:** no longer shown after every update. It appears on the very first launch, when "show on launch"
  is on, or when audio access is missing.
- **Update check:** finds the installer among GitHub's release files (GitHub turns spaces into dots), so Download
  Installer is offered instead of only the release page.

## Under the hood
- Code split into smaller files, and settings keys named in one place; one shared log-frequency axis. Measurements are
  bit-identical to build 118's in the reference tests.
- New automated checks: reference analysis results, the snapshot layout, the graphics shaders, 163 model checks with
  simulated devices and plugins, and the capture recovery checks.
- `--capture-selftest` also tests a capture restart.

## Not yet verified
Build 120 passed every automated check that needs no audio hardware. It has not been tested with real audio
interfaces, output capture, sample-rate changes, unplugging, sleep and wake, or in a DAW. That is this build's purpose.

## Installing
Use `Auraliscope 2.0 rc 3 build 120.pkg` and choose the app, VST3 and/or Audio Unit.
- **Replaces:** any 2.0 build and earlier Bridge plugins, in place.
- **Keeps:** your settings and presets.
- **Requirements:** the app needs an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later; the plugins are universal.

Quit Auraliscope and your DAW first, and rescan plugins in the DAW afterwards.
