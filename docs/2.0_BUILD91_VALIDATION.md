## Boundaries

The installer was expanded and verified, not installed. No Developer ID identity is configured: binaries are ad hoc
signed, the installer is unsigned, and this release is not notarized. No broad real-DAW music-session, Intel hardware,
older-macOS, long-session thermal/power or Save-panel interaction validation is claimed. PDF review used the synthetic
demo and direct export diagnostics. A diagnostic relaunch initially had no visible window until app activation;
the subsequent Dark capture and report export completed. The full GUI lifecycle suite was not run.

Dialogue gating remains unavailable. ARIB's current operative limits were not accessible in its public sample and
are treated as a reference. Other service presets retain build 90 values; they are not a certification of current
service delivery contracts. Mastering thresholds need interpretation with real material.

Logs and screenshots are retained locally in `.build/release-validation/`; a validation-log archive accompanies the
release downloads. Earlier BUILDxx test notes describe historical builds only.
